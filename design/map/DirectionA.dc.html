Direction A

The atlas

Real coastlines, drawn as hairlines on cream. Regions are washes of colour that deepen with how many bottles you've had.

Why it works. You learn where a region actually is — that Alto Piemonte sits under the Alps and Barolo doesn't. Geography is the thing that makes the names stick.

The cost. Real borders need real shapes. Small appellations are a few pixels at phone width, so it only works once you've zoomed into a country.
