The tile map

No coastlines. Every region is a tile, placed roughly where it belongs and shaded by how many bottles you've had from it.

Dashed — regions you haven't logged yet

Why it works. Every name is legible at every zoom, which is the actual job — you are trying to learn words. It also shows the gaps: the regions next to the ones you like.

The cost. It isn't a map. You'd learn that Langhe and Alto Piemonte are both in Piemonte without ever learning that one is an hour from the Alps and the other isn't.
