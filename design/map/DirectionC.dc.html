Direction C

The plate

One country per screen, drawn as a flat silhouette. Regions are bands across it, named in the margin like an engraved plate in a wine atlas.

The north Piemonte · Lombardia · Veneto · Friuli 6 of your 10
The centre Toscana · Umbria · Marche 1 bottle
The south Campania · Puglia · Sicilia None yet

Why it works. It answers your actual question — "which bit of northern Italy?" — on one screen, with the names set large enough to read and remember.

The cost. There's no world view. You'd arrive here from a country list rather than by pinching out to see everywhere you've drunk from at once.
